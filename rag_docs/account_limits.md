# Account limits (KwikPay support policy, synthetic)

A basic wallet can hold up to GHS 5,000 and send up to GHS 2,000 per day. A verified wallet linked to a Ghana Card can hold up to GHS 20,000 and send up to GHS 10,000 per day.

To raise your limit, upgrade your wallet in the KwikPay app using your own Ghana Card. We never ask for card numbers in chat.

Limits reset at midnight, Ghana time.
