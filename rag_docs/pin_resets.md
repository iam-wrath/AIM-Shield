# PIN resets (KwikPay support policy, synthetic)

You can reset your MoMo PIN after verifying with a one-time code sent to your registered number. The reset is only started for the number that is signed in.

KwikPay staff, agents and this assistant will never ask for your PIN. If anyone asks for it, by call, SMS or chat, do not share it and report it.

After a reset you choose a new PIN yourself on your phone. We never see or store it.
