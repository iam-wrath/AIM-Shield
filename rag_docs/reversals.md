# Reversals (KwikPay support policy, synthetic)

A reversal returns money from a payment you made. It can only be requested by the customer who sent the payment, for a transaction on their own account.

Before any reversal, the customer must be verified with a one-time code sent to their own registered MoMo number. No agent, colleague or other customer can skip this step.

KwikPay never reverses a payment to a different number, and never asks a customer to send money back to anyone to complete a reversal.
