# Fees (KwikPay support policy, synthetic)

Sending money to another KwikPay wallet is free up to GHS 100 per day, then 0.5 percent. Cash out at an agent costs 1 percent.

Failed transfers are never charged. If a fee was taken on a failed transfer, it is returned with the amount.

Fees are shown before you confirm. If the amount shown differs from the amount taken, contact support with the transaction ID.
